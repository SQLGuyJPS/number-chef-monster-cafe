# Shop economy simulation

Generated by `npm run economy:sim` from `src/engine/economy.ts` (catalog, D148) and `src/engine/economySim.ts`. Later content is planned counts (D149). Each figure is the median of 300 seeded runs, with the 10th–90th percentile in brackets.

## Assumptions

- Play: 60 min a week, in sittings of 15–20 min; a 6-order service takes 3–4 min, plus 0.5 min of Results and menu between services and 1.5 min in the shop at the end of a sitting that has something to buy.
- A service starts only with ≥ 2 min of the sitting left (and then is finished). Requeues: one in 20% of services, two in 5%. 5% of services are closed early (no tip).
- Earning is SPEC §9 unchanged: 3 coins a monster, +5 tip for a finished service (23 for a plain 6-order service).
- Tier prices: T1 20 · T2 30 · T3 55 · T4 100 · T5 180. A second or third colorway costs 75% of the item (rounded to 5); colorways of a free default cost T1.
- Spending habits: **impulse** buys something random she can afford, up to 2 a sitting; **cheapest** buys the cheapest things first, as many as she can; **saver** picks a wish and buys it as soon as she can (the wish-item milestone).
